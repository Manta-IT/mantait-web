FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between Web Quick and Web Standard?

Web Quick is a simple business card website. A page about the company, services, contact, possibly references. Text and image changes are made on request. A good fit if you run a small company or a trade and the website mainly serves as your business card.

Web Standard has easy content management - you edit texts and images and add articles or references yourself. Larger scope, search engine optimization, Google Analytics. A good fit for companies that want to update the website regularly.

Who are these websites for?

Web Quick suits tradespeople, sole traders, and small companies. A flower shop, a plumber, a lawyer, a beauty salon, a small trading company.

Web Standard suits small and mid-sized companies that need a serious website. Distributors, manufacturers, services with employees, non-profits.

Do you use AI? How does it affect the result?

Yes, I use AI to generate design, code, and text drafts. I manage the whole website myself, and AI saves me the time I would otherwise pay a team for. That is why it is done fast and at a fraction of the usual price.

Every website still goes through my review. I fine-tune the structure, design, and key elements by hand. AI does not decide for me. The result is your custom website, not a template.

What about the domain and hosting? Where will the website run?

I host websites on Cloudflare or a similarly modern service. The hosting is fast, secure, and often free in the basic tier. The account is created in your name - both the hosting and the domain belong to you, not me.

If you have an existing domain (e.g. firma.cz), I will help with the switch. If you still need a domain, I will recommend one and help register it. Domain registration (approx. 13 EUR per year) is not included in the website price.

What if I want to extend or change the website later?

Small changes I handle as we go. Larger extensions are quoted by scope, typically 1-2 days of work. The website does not run on an expensive platform with monthly fees - changes cost you nothing extra.

If your website grows over time and you need a full-featured one, we can move to Web Standard - the structure and the domain stay.

Who owns the website, and what if we part ways?

You do. The domain and hosting are registered in your name and you receive the code. If we part ways, the website keeps running and anyone else can take it over. I commit in writing to transfer the account with the website within 5 working days whenever you ask. You are not locked in by contract or technology.

What does running the website cost?

Almost nothing. Hosting at this size is free or a few euros a year, the domain costs approx. 13 EUR per year. No monthly fees for a CMS, templates or plugins. You pay once for the build, not forever for the operation.

What does the website comply with?

Mandatory legal information. Name, registered office, company ID and registry record as required by Czech law (the website targets customers in the Czech Republic).

Personal data done properly. The contact form has correct data processing wording. And because the website uses no tracking cookies, it needs no cookie banner.

Speed and mobile. Fonts and maps are part of the website; nothing loads from third parties. It opens within two seconds, even on a phone.

Accessibility. Readable font sizes, sufficient contrast, keyboard navigation.

Search engine basics. Page descriptions, structured data, a sitemap. People find you by "your trade + your town".

My domain is held by the company that built my old website. Is that a problem?

No. The domain is yours even if someone else registered it - we transfer it to you, or simply point it at the new website. I handle it; you only approve the transfer. And if you have no domain at all, we choose and register one in your name together.
