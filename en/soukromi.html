Forms on this website

Data you send me yourself.

The contact form and the grant eligibility questionnaire collect your name, e-mail, phone number, company ID, the municipality where the project takes place, and the details about your company that you fill in (size, planned project, estimated investment, status of obligations towards public authorities, ownership ties). I ask for them so I can assess whether you qualify for a grant; in the fields about obligations and ownership ties, "yes / working on it / don't know" is enough, I do not want any amounts or details from you.

  • Purpose: to assess your enquiry, respond to it and possibly agree on working together.
  • Legal basis: steps towards a contract that you yourself have shown interest in (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR), and subsequently performance of the contract and accounting and tax obligations.
  • How long: I delete non-binding enquiries 24 months after the last contact. For completed engagements, statutory retention periods for accounting documents apply (10 years).
Reaching out to companies

When I write to you.

I contact companies with an offer that is relevant to them, for example when a grant call with an approaching deadline is open in their region. I look up the contact details of the managing director or the company address in public sources: the commercial register, the register of economic entities and company websites.

  • Purpose: to offer a service the company otherwise has no way of learning about.
  • Legal basis: legitimate interest (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR) in offering my own services to businesses. I have assessed that this is ordinary B2B communication to a work contact, on a small scale and without intruding on privacy.
  • How long: I keep the contact for at most 24 months; if you reply that you do not wish to hear from me, I add it permanently to my list of addresses I never contact again.
  • How to stop it: reply to any of my e-mails with the word STOP, or write to petr.kokoska@mantait.cz. I will stop immediately and will not ask why.
