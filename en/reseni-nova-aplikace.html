How it works

The brief takes shape as we go. You just answer.

1

You describe what you need

In plain sentences, the way you'd tell a colleague. Send the materials as you have them, Excel is fine. I ask about the rest, put the brief together from your answers myself and give you a fixed price.

2

First version within a week

Not a presentation or a sketch. A working application you can open and click through. You write down what doesn't fit, and the next day you have an updated version.

3

I deploy and train

I get the application into your company, set up the accounts and coordinate with your IT. You don't need to know more than that it works.

What you get

A finished thing, not a project.

01

Price stated up front

A fixed amount for the agreed scope. Anything beyond it gets a price in advance and you decide.

02

Fine-tuning included

If you find while using it that something is missing or should behave differently, I fix it. That is not extra work, that is part of the handover.

03

One person for everything

You write to the one person who builds it. Nothing is relayed through anyone else.

04

An application that is yours

It runs at your place or wherever you want. You don't pay a monthly fee just for it to run, and you are not tied to one supplier.

05

You don't depend on me

At handover you get the source code and instructions on how to run the application. Any other programmer can take it over anytime without starting from scratch. Nothing of yours is locked in with me.

06

I run it, or hand it over

Hosting, domain, maintenance and security: I take care of them, or hand them over to your person. You choose what suits you and can change your mind anytime.
