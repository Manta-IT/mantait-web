Case study

Integrating Raynet and Pohoda

Client Distribution company
Role Product Owner on the client side

Situation: The company used Raynet for sales and Pohoda, a Czech accounting system, for bookkeeping. Salespeople retyped customers, quotes, and orders between the two systems by hand. Slow, error-prone, frustrating.

What I did: I defined what exactly the integration should do and for whom - from the product, process, and business perspective. I decided on the architecture (why an integration app rather than a simple script or an external automation). I found a supplier, commissioned the development, and tested the result. After delivery, I wrote the manuals, trained the salespeople, and ran the rollout workshops. I left the programming to developers and managed everything else myself.

Result: Sales and accounting in one world. No retyping, no errors. Management sees the customers' financial status directly in Raynet. Salespeople actually use the solution because they understand it - I designed the training that way. I can repeat the same approach for you.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Who is Raynet a good fit for?

Raynet is a Czech CRM built mainly for small and mid-sized companies in the Czech Republic and Slovakia. It suits trading companies, service businesses, distributors, and companies with B2B sales.

It is affordable, localized, and connects to Czech accounting systems (Pohoda, Money, Helios) and invoicing services. It knows local tax, legal, and business practices first-hand.

What can you solve in Raynet?

I manage the whole job except the programming itself. Setup from scratch, accounting integration (I have hands-on experience with Raynet and Pohoda), custom forms and processes, audits of existing setups, and training for salespeople and management.

If you have Raynet but see little benefit from it - salespeople avoid it, reports make no sense, the accounting connection is missing - I can find out where it is stuck and propose next steps.

I take your project on as my own. I define what is needed. Where coding is required, I find a supplier. I test the result and make sure your people can actually use it.
