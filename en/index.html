FAQ

Common questions

Do I need to understand the technology?

No. You describe what slows you down and what you want to decide about; I translate between you and the technology. I explain everything so you can decide with confidence, not blindly.

How long does it take?

The AI plan is ready within 48 hours of the second workshop. The first version of a custom application within a week. A website within one to two weeks. For larger projects I give you an exact date after the first call, not a guess over the phone.

What happens to our data?

Your data stays in your systems; I do not copy it to mine. For AI I choose vendors that do not use your data to train their models. Personal data processing follows GDPR, with a written agreement where needed.

What does it cost?

The price list is right here on this page - nothing is hidden in a quote. Fixed prices are agreed in advance; for "from" items you know the exact price after the first meeting. I am not a VAT payer, prices are final.

What if it does not work?

I tell you in advance where AI or a new system will not help, and I leave those places out of the plan. You pay for an agreed scope, and fine-tuning what is in it is on me. When something gets stuck, I deal with it - not you.

Not sure which box you fit in?

Call me. Fifteen minutes on the phone is usually enough for me to tell you whether I can help or not. You are not ordering anything and I will not show up until you want me to.
