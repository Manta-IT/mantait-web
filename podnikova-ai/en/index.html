The machine prepares, a person signs

The platform reads email and your systems and prepares a draft reply, a supplier chaser or a price entry. Nothing goes out until a person confirms it under their own name.

  • The draft reply waits in Outlook in the Drafts folder, the price entry in the CRM. You confirm, edit or reject it with one click.
  • Nothing falls through. After 24 hours a reminder arrives, after 48 the item goes to a deputy. Above CZK 100k, the managing director approves.

It answers from your documents and shows the source

The platform searches drawings, quotes, price lists and invoices. Every answer shows where it came from, and when it cannot find a source, it says so.

  • Numbers from the source. Dimensions, drawing numbers and prices come straight from the document and are never estimated.

Frequently asked questions

What is local enterprise AI?

A language model and agents that run on your company's machine or on a machine rented just for you. They work with email, CRM, accounting and documents, prepare routine work and answer questions. Every person sees only what they are allowed to open in the source systems.

Is it running anywhere yet?

No. The prototype shows what the platform will look like and how people work in it. I am looking for the first companies to get it running with. The company and the data in the demo are fictional.

Why local?

A question about a contract or a drawing stays inside your perimeter. The model runs on a machine that is yours or rented just for you, and the running cost does not depend on the number of questions.

How does the platform know who may see what?

It takes permissions from the source: Microsoft 365 groups, folder permissions and roles in the CRM and accounting. It does not invent its own list, so when you remove a permission at the source, it disappears from the platform at the next sync.

We already run our own local chat. Why a platform?

A chat over a model is a good start. The platform adds permissions on documents, workflows that prepare work on their own, and an overview for management. If your model fits, it can stay.

Which systems does it connect to?

The ones you have. The prototype works with RAYNET, HubSpot, Pipedrive, Salesforce, POHODA, HELIOS, ABRA, SAP Business One, iDoklad, Fakturoid and Microsoft 365. Another system connects the same way, through a standard interface.

Which model, and under which licence?

We choose the model by workflow, document language and a licence your industry allows. It runs on your machine, so it can be swapped later without changing workflows or permissions.

What does running it cost?

A monthly oversight fee based on the number of workflows and systems, with no per-question and no per-user fee. On top of that, either your own server with a graphics card, bought once, or the cloud version as one monthly item.

Does our data have to be ready?

No. If it is not, we start with a two-week company preparation. The AI plan tells you whether it is needed.

Can it be co-funded by subsidies?

Sometimes. Servers, software and implementation are often eligible costs in Czech regional vouchers or OP TAK calls. Whether that applies to you, we check in the AI plan.
