02 — why a dedicated interface

Three things email can't hold

The most common objection: just send it to me by email with a button and I don't need a system. It almost works. It breaks on these three things.

  1. Who is asking email can't carry it

    For AI to answer only from what that person is allowed to open, it has to know who is asking at the moment of the question. A shared inbox, a forward or a delegate breaks that chain.

    And the same rule that says incoming mail cannot instruct the agent also says incoming mail cannot authenticate anyone — otherwise a forward would be a privilege escalation.

  2. What happened inboxes get cleaned out

    Who approved, what sources it came from, which model and prompt version, and when. Plus a mark that a machine wrote it — the EU AI regulation has required that since August 2026.

    A decision can't be reconstructed from email half a year later. And when someone asks, they usually ask half a year later.

  3. What is pending email has no state

    Owner, deadline, delegate, reminders, retry on error, an exception queue and a kill switch for every process separately.

    Email can announce. It cannot remember that nobody clicked something for nine days — and that is exactly the most common silent failure of the whole system.

Without a dedicated layer you can build AI that sees only what absolutely everyone in the company is allowed to see. That's a ceiling, not an option. But the layer is not a workplace — nine times out of ten, things end up where people already work.

05 — what it does in your systems

One record, four systems, one stop

The most common worry: what will this do to what I already have. This is the whole path of one incoming invoice. Watch where it stops.

Mail
Platform
CRM
Accounting
  1. The supplier sends an invoice by email arrives in a dedicated inbox, not the company's general mail
  2. The amount, number and due date are extracted read-only, into a fixed schema — the email text goes no further
  3. The order is found and the difference is calculated read-only: ordered 42,800, invoiced 43,690 — 2% more
  4. It stops here. The buyer confirms the difference. the only place in the whole chain where it waits for a person
  5. The invoice is created a record noting who confirmed it and when
  6. A link to the document is added to the order a record; nothing is overwritten, only added
Today, a person does this in companies by retyping.
No write ever goes out without human confirmation. Except reads and internal notes.
Nothing is deleted or overwritten. Only added.
Every record carries who prepared it — the platform — and who confirmed it.
When a write fails, it stays in the queue with a link to the record. It doesn't disappear.

In every one of your systems it stays clear what is human work and what the platform prepared — even for someone who never opens the portal.

11 — how you know it's working

Company flow and deadlines come first

Quote, offer, order, invoice and delivery: how many are open, how long they take to move through the company, and who they're waiting on. This is the main measure for management.

AI usage and the edit rate on its text are only a second layer. Beside them stays a safety criterion that must sit at zero: how many times did someone get content they had no right to.
